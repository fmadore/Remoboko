Remoboko interactive figures

Maps, charts and a timeline from Religion, Morality and Boko in West Africa, a Leibniz Junior Research Group (2018–2024) at Leibniz-Zentrum Moderner Orient, Berlin, that studied religiosity and secular education on university campuses in Togo, Benin, Niger and Nigeria. Every figure reads its data from this repository and can be embedded in a page.

From the book

  • Points of interest around Abomey-Calavi and Lomé

    33 churches, mosques, schools, universities and campus landmarks, searchable and filterable by country.

  • Universities of Benin and Togo

    The four public universities studied in the book, with their logos.

  • Religion, education and politics in Togo and Benin, 1960–2010

    37 dated events, filterable by theme.

From the final report

  • Collaborators by country

    93 collaborators across 24 countries, with names on hover.

  • Collaborators by affiliation

    The same people located at 56 institutions on a world map.

  • Publications and activities by type and language

    181 outputs as a zoomable treemap: type, then language, then year.

  • Publications and activities over time

    The same outputs stacked by type, per quarter or per year, 2018–2025.

Book: Frédérick Madore, Religious Activism on Campuses in Togo and Benin (De Gruyter, 2025), doi:10.1515/9783111428895.
